    What this is

    The working site of one project: to build a Mac app with the features of KaleidaGraph and the window of the TRAE IDE, for the laboratory practices of the Physics degree, with a live bench mode where the student's readings become plots and fits as they are typed. It holds the brief, the study of KaleidaGraph, the design, the seed-place of ideas, a dated log of every step, the release notes and the manuals of the core and the app.
