I012026-10-17, Josudiscussed→ module

The Live Bench: the student is the instrument, and the plots and fits follow every keystroke

A work mode that is like performing an experiment connected to an instrument, where the instrument is the student at the laboratory bench retrieving data from the experiment and introducing them in the app. As soon as a datum enters, the app does whatever it has been programmed to do: the plots are directly created and synced, and if there are fits those are also done. A work mode of its own. Said with the brief: the app is for the practices of Técnicas Experimentales I–IV, where a student reads an apparatus (a balance, a chronometer, a voltmeter, a ruler, a thermometer) and writes numbers down; today those numbers go to a sheet of paper or a spreadsheet, and the plot and the fit come at the end, when a mistake can no longer be remeasured.

2026-10-17decision Josu: the template decides whether expected values are shown (hidden by default); Ley de Hooke is the first practice. The Bench's form taken as the draft proposed, a module whose view can take the whole window. The Bench is the brief's own (R05); phase 3 builds it.

I022026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)green→ module

Practice templates: the teacher prepares the file, the student opens it and measures

A practice of Técnicas Experimentales as a file of the app: the columns with their names, units and instrument uncertainties; the plots already laid out; the fits already defined with the model and the parameters' meanings; the derived quantities (a formula from the fitted slope to the physical constant); the questions the report must answer. The teacher writes it once, from a blank project or from last year's; the student opens it, and the Bench (I01) is ready to receive the first reading. The same file, after the session, holds the student's data, fits and answers, and is what is handed in.

2026-10-17decision Green light from Josu for version 1: appended to the brief as R13 (practice templates); a stream will take it in its phase.

I032026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)green→ core

Uncertainties and units in every column, propagated through formulas and into weighted fits

Every column carries a unit and an uncertainty (the instrument's resolution, a typed value, a column of its own, or none), as the practices teach from the first day. A formula on columns propagates units (and refuses a sum of metres and seconds) and uncertainties (by the usual linear propagation; later by sampling); a fit on columns with uncertainties is weighted by them, and its \(\chi^2\) per degree of freedom tells whether the uncertainties were honest; an axis is labelled with the column's name and unit without typing. KaleidaGraph has error bars and a weighted fit but no units and no propagation; a spreadsheet has neither.

2026-10-17decision Green light from Josu for version 1: appended to the brief as R14 (units and uncertainties in every column); a stream will take it in its phase.

I042026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)green→ core

The visible pipeline: data to plot to fit as a graph the student can read

Everything in a project is a node that depends on others: a column on its cells or its formula, a plot on its columns, a fit on its plot's variables, a derived value on a fit's parameter, a figure of the report on a plot. The core keeps the graph and recomputes along it when something changes; that is what makes the Bench (I01) live and KaleidaGraph's Update Plot unnecessary. The graph is also shown: a small diagram in the sidebar of what feeds what, so that a student sees that the slope in the report comes from this fit of this plot of these columns, and a teacher sees what a template (I02) wires.

I052026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)green→ module

The lab report exported: figures, tables and the fit's results, by the family's figure rules

The report of a practice (a document the student writes, in Quarto, LaTeX, Word or Pages) takes its figures and tables from the project: the plots as PDF, SVG or PNG with every character typeset by LaTeX, identical geometry across a series and one colour per meaning, as diff-ref-struct's Publish module does under Josu's ThesisFigures rules; the fits' parameters with their uncertainties as a table; the data as CSV; a skeleton of the report with those pieces in place and the questions of the template (I02) as headings. Everything regenerated from the project, so that a figure never disagrees with the table beside it.

I062026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)seed→ extension

The teacher's class view: every bench's live data on one screen

In a laboratory session, each group's project sends its state (not its screen) to the teacher's Mac on the local network: a board with one card per bench, the series' length, the fit's state, a flag where a reading is far off; and, at the end, every group's value of the same constant on one histogram, which is the best lesson on uncertainty there is. Nothing leaves the room; the teacher's Mac runs the same app with a Class module.

I072026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)seed→ core

Sessions recorded as they happen, and replayed

Every reading committed at the Bench is written with its time to a file of the project, a lab book the app keeps by itself: what was typed, corrected, remeasured, in what order. The session can be replayed at its own pace or faster, which is a tutorial (I12), a way to check a group's work, and the "instrument emulation" the brief's image suggests: the app played a recorded experiment as if the readings were arriving now.

I082026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)seed→ app

Entry made for one hand on the apparatus

At the bench the other hand holds the chronometer. Entry must work from the numeric keypad alone: a number, Return, the next cell; Tab across; the last reading repeated with one key; the last reading undone with one key; a reading typed with its unit ("12.3 cm") converted to the column's unit; the decimal comma accepted as the decimal point (the students' keyboards are Spanish); the next cell always visible, large, with the column's name and unit; and the app saying, by a sound or a colour, that the reading was taken and whether it looks right. No dialog ever opens during a series.

I092026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)seed→ extension

The phone as the second keyboard at the bench

A QR code on the Bench opens a plain web page on the partner's phone, served by the app on the local network: a keypad for the next reading, nothing else. One student reads the apparatus and types on the phone, the other watches the plot grow on the Mac. Later, the phone's own sensors (its clock, its accelerometer, its microphone) as sources of readings, with their uncertainties declared.

I102026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)seed→ core

Formula Entry with named columns, and KaleidaGraph's files read

KaleidaGraph's Formula Entry addresses columns as c0, c1… and registers as m0…; here columns have names (time, position, voltage) and formulas read as physics (velocity = diff(position) / diff(time)), with the units checked (I03). KaleidaGraph's own notation stays accepted, so that its library files (the function and curve-fit definitions shipped as text), its macro sources and its formula scripts can be imported, and a KaleidaGraph user's habits carry over. Its binary files (.qda, .qpc, .qpl) are read only if a specification is found; its text exports always.

2026-10-17said Proposed by the session; the compatibility's extent is design question Q3.

I112026-10-17, the session (proposed to Josu)seed→ core

Every fit checked against Python and KaleidaGraph, and reported as the practices ask

The core's fits (linear with and without weights, polynomial, the general nonlinear fit) are tested against SciPy's curve_fit and against KaleidaGraph's own results on the example files shipped with the demo, to the digits KaleidaGraph prints; the parameters' uncertainties come from the covariance matrix with the convention the practices use (and the convention stated, since KaleidaGraph's "parameter errors" and SciPy's differ in how they scale with \(\chi^2\)). The fit's report is what a lab report needs: parameters with uncertainties and units, \(\chi^2\)/ν, R\(^{2}\), the residuals plotted under the fit, the confidence band.

2026-10-17work Done for the straight line: KaleidaGraph's fit1 on the Hooke data, read off the demo, equals the core to every printed digit (test kaleidagraph); its errors are the scatter convention. The presets' logarithm base and the Linear fit's View Results remain to be read with the app active.

On this page

    What this is

    The working site of one project: to build a Mac app with the features of KaleidaGraph and the window of the TRAE IDE, for the laboratory practices of the Physics degree, with a live bench mode where the student's readings become plots and fits as they are typed. It holds the brief, the study of KaleidaGraph, the design, the seed-place of ideas, a dated log of every step, the release notes and the manuals of the core and the app.
